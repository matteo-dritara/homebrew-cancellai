Show disk usage and cleanup candidates (default command)

Usage: cancellai-cli status [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --days <DAYS>                Retention cutoff in days [default: 7]
      --keep-latest <KEEP_LATEST>  Always protect the N most-recently-modified sessions per tool, independent of age [default: 2]
      --tool <TOOL>                Restrict to one provider [default: all] [possible values: all, codex, claude]
      --json                       Machine-readable output
      --allow-running              Proceed even though a Codex/Claude process appears to be running, or liveness could not be determined
  -h, --help                       Print help
