# ADR-0010: Open local core, commercial fleet plane

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-27
- Related: PD-008, PD-002

## Context

Trust benefits from an inspectable local core, while sustainable commercial value is more natural at team/fleet scale.

## Decision

Single-machine scanner, safety kernel, provider SDK, policy, quarantine, Guardian, CLI and TUI remain open source. Future paid services may coordinate policies, identity, audit and visibility across many nodes.

## Consequences

No cloud subscription is required for complete local safety/governance. Fleet services cannot become a privileged remote-delete bypass.
